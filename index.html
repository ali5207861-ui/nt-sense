About NT-Sense

An ISEF research project. NT-Sense estimates NT-proBNP, a heart-failure blood test, from a 10-second ECG plus routine patient information, and gives a range that shows how sure it is.

12-lead ECG
AUROC 0.937 at 300 pg/mL
Lead II only
AUROC 0.919 at 300 pg/mL
90% range
held the real value 90.1% of the time
Test set
4,191 tests from 2,654 patients never seen in training

Model trained on MIMIC-IV-ECG; encoder pretrained on 114k ECG machine reports; the full model adds routine blood tests, history and a previous NT-proBNP; ranges by conformal prediction. Monitor mode also tested on MIMIC-III bedside monitors. Demo-case ECGs: MIMIC-IV-ECG, open data (ODbL). Runs entirely in your browser: the ECG you add is never uploaded. Online version for trying the demo cases.

Research prototype. Not approved for clinical decisions. Always confirm with a laboratory test.
